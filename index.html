Scan Your Microsoft 365 (Read-Only)

Sign in with a Microsoft 365 admin account and approve read-only access. ZeroWaste IT can't change, delete or send anything. Your results are shown once and deleted from our server within 15 minutes.

What we read: users and their licenses, last sign-in dates, Microsoft 365 usage reports, and which third-party apps people have connected. The first time, a Global Administrator approves this for your organization.
